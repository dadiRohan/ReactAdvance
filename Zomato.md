# Zomato Cloud Kitchen Steps
To start a home-based cloud kitchen and sell your food online through Zomato, you must complete a digital onboarding process. Zomato fully allows home-based cloud kitchens, provided you have a mandatory food license and business paperwork.  
Here is the step-by-step breakdown of the documentation, registration process, and how to start accepting orders:
## 📋 Phase 1: Keep Mandatory Documents Ready
Zomato will not approve your home kitchen without valid government licenses. Gather these first:

* FSSAI License: Absolute mandatory 14-digit registration number. If your yearly revenue is expected to be under ₹12 Lakhs, you only need a Basic FSSAI Registration (costs ₹100/year, takes 7–10 days, apply online at FoSCoS FSSAI Portal).
* PAN Card: Your personal PAN card functions perfectly to start.
* Bank Account Details: A cancelled cheque or copy of your bank passbook to clear weekly online payouts.
* GST Registration Certificate: Optional if your annual turnover is under ₹20 Lakhs, but mandatory if you scale past that limit.

## 💻 Phase 2: The Zomato Online Registration Process
Once your documents are scanned, complete the application online:

   1. Visit the Partner Portal: Go to the [Zomato Partner Page](https://www.zomato.com/partner-with-us/new/) and click "Register Your Restaurant".
   2. Fill Basic Details: Enter your name, phone number, city, and your home cloud kitchen name.
   3. Select Service Type: Choose "Food Delivery Only" (since it is a cloud kitchen with no dine-in option).
   4. Upload Documents: Submit your FSSAI number, PAN, and banking information.
   5. Upload Menu & Photos: Input your dish names, descriptions, prices, and clear, bright pictures of your actual food items.
   6. Sign the Digital Contract: Review and electronically sign the partnership agreement. Zomato charges a one-time onboarding fee (typically around ₹1,800) and takes a commission on every order (generally 15% to 30%). 

## 🚀 Phase 3: Going Live & Handling Orders
After submission, Zomato will verify your registration within 24 to 48 hours (sometimes up to 5-7 days). Once approved, you will receive a WhatsApp and email confirmation. Here is how you manage the online workflow:

* Download the App: Install the Zomato Restaurant Partner App on your smartphone or log into their web dashboard.
* Set Kitchen Timings: Define your operational hours inside the app so customers know when they can order from your home.
* Go Online: On your app dashboard, tap the status toggle to change it from "Offline" to "Online". Your home kitchen will instantly become visible to users within your delivery radius.
* Process Orders: When a customer places an order, your phone will ring with a notification. Accept the order, prepare the food fresh in your home kitchen, and pack it safely.
* Handover over to Rider: A Zomato delivery partner will automatically be assigned to your kitchen, arrive at your home door, pick up the package, and deliver it.
